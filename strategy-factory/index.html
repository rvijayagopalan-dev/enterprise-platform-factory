Strategy governance principles

Clear decision rights

Name the accountable decision-maker, consulted stakeholders, approval forum and review date for each material choice.

Evidence and provenance

Distinguish observed facts, estimates, assumptions and recommendations; link claims to source, owner and freshness.

Responsible use of AI

AI supports synthesis and option exploration. Leaders validate evidence, assess bias and retain accountability for decisions.
