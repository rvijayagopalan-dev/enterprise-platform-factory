Policy enforcement points

Data classification, approved purpose, model and region constraints
Blueprint & architecture gate
Approved patterns, threat model, architecture standards
Build & supply-chain gate
SAST, SCA, secret scan, SBOM, provenance and signed artifacts
Infrastructure plan gate
OPA / policy-as-code, drift, budget and region restrictions
Production deployment gate
Human approval, change window, rollback and separation of duties
Runtime continuous evaluation
Posture, cost, SLO, threat and policy drift
