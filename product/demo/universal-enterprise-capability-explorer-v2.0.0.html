Modeling rule

Commonality ≠ identical hierarchy

Reuse a definition only when the underlying ability and outcome are semantically the same. Otherwise use a contextual specialization, extension or separate capability.

Avoid generic phase trees such as strategy → execution → compliance → performance repeated under every industry domain.

Composition integrity

Never silently assume

  • Unassessed is not applicable.
  • Missing mapping is not universal.
  • Industry overlays preserve lineage.
  • Duplicates require semantic resolution.
  • Human review precedes publication.

Capability-to-factory realization

Capabilities and factories are separate entities. A factory may realize many capabilities; a capability may be realized by several factories or shared services.
