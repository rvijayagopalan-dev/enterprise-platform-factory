A single governed lifecycle
01 · Idea intake & discovery — EMF + EAIP

Capture the idea, identify stakeholders, classify intent, retrieve enterprise context, challenge assumptions and formulate a value hypothesis.

02 · Capability & product blueprint — EMF

Map universal and industry capabilities, assess maturity, discover gaps, create product requirements, ontologies, architecture and factory specifications.

03 · Human-AI operating design — HAE

Assign roles, authority, decision rights, human-in-loop checkpoints, escalation paths, workforce skills and accountability.

04 · Plan & orchestrate — EAIP

Decompose work, select agents and tools, build context, coordinate A2A/MCP interactions, manage memory, policy and evaluations.

05 · Engineer & provision — EPF + specialized factories

Generate infrastructure, applications, data products, integrations, tests, security controls, CI/CD and deployment assets.

06 · Operate & optimize — EAIP + EPF + HAE

Observe services and agents, manage incidents, evaluate quality, control cost, supervise autonomy and continuously improve.

07 · Outcomes & value realization — Enterprise Value Office

Compare baseline to actual results, validate benefits, attribute value, report confidence and feed learning into the next portfolio cycle.
